# 🛒 Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

This project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to **track KPIs, compare year-over-year performance, and uncover actionable business insights** from ecommerce sales data. 
It is designed as a **portfolio piece for recruiters and stakeholders**, showcasing both technical skills and business storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Audience
- **Audience:** Recruiters, hiring managers, and business stakeholders evaluating data analysis and visualization skills  
- **Purpose:** To highlight proficiency in Tableau, KPI tracking, calculated field logic, and clear communication of business insights  
- **Outcome:** Demonstrates the ability to translate raw data into **decision-ready dashboards**

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
1. **How did overall sales, profit, and order volume perform in 2017 compared to 2016?**  
   → KPI line charts with YoY percentage changes  

2. **Which regions contributed most to sales and market share?**  
   → Doughnut chart showing distribution across USCA, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Oceania  

3. **Which product categories grew or declined year-over-year?**  
   → Horizontal bar chart comparing YTD vs PYTD sales across categories (men's footweatr, Camping & Hiking, Golf, etc.)  

4. **What were the customer acquisition trends across markets?**  
   → Bubble chart visualizing monthly order growth by region  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Sales Growth:** Overall sales increased in 2017, with notable gains in Latam and Europe  
- **Category Performance:** Water Sports and Camping & Hiking showed strong YoY growth, while Golf declined  
- **Customer Acquisition:** Latam and Asia drove the highest new customer orders, indicating expanding market reach  
- **Profit Trends:** Profit margins improved in Europe but remained flat in Africa, suggesting regional pricing or cost challenges 
- **Market Share:** Latam & Europe dominates the market while Africa has the least share, suggesting regional pricing or cost challenges 

---

## ⚙️ Techniques Used
- Dynamic filters and calculated fields for year selection  
- KPI comparison using YoY percentage formulas  
- Chart types: line, doughnut, horizontal bar, bubble chart  
- Color-coded trends and tooltips for clarity  
- Interactive layout for stakeholder-friendly storytelling
