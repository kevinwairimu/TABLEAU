# ✈️ British Airways Review Dashboard

This Tableau dashboard analyzes **customer entertainment reviews for British Airways (2017–2022)**. It explores satisfaction trends across aircraft types, countries, and time, helping identify areas for **service improvement** and **customer experience optimization**. Designed as a **portfolio project**, it demonstrates skills in data visualization, storytelling, and actionable insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Audience
- **Audience:** Recruiters, hiring managers, and business stakeholders interested in customer experience analytics  
- **Purpose:** Showcase ability to transform review data into clear, interactive dashboards  
- **Outcome:** Demonstrates proficiency in Tableau, geographic storytelling, and aircraft‑level performance analysis  

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
1. **How have entertainment ratings changed over time (2017–2022)?**  
   → Line chart showing monthly average ratings  

2. **Which countries report the highest and lowest entertainment satisfaction?**  
   → Map visualization of average scores by country  

3. **Which aircraft types deliver the best entertainment experience?**  
   → Bar chart comparing average ratings across aircraft (e.g., Boeing 747, Airbus A380)  

4. **How do review volumes vary by aircraft type?**  
   → Horizontal bar chart showing number of reviews per aircraft  

5. **How do traveller type, cabin flown, and other filters affect satisfaction scores?**  
   → Interactive filters for dynamic exploration  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Overall Ratings:** Entertainment averages 3.6/5, slightly higher than other service metrics (seat comfort, staff service, food, etc.)  
- **Aircraft Comparison:** Larger aircraft (e.g., Airbus A380) tend to receive higher entertainment ratings compared to older models  
- **Country Trends:** Certain regions consistently report lower satisfaction, highlighting opportunities for localized service improvements  
- **Time Series:** Ratings fluctuate across years, with noticeable dips in 2020–2021, likely linked to pandemic disruptions  

---

## ⚙️ Techniques Used
- Data blending with country metadata  
- Calculated fields for average ratings  
- Map visualizations for geographic insights  
- Dual bar charts for aircraft comparison  
- Interactive filters for traveller type, cabin, and date range  
- Time series analysis for trend detection
