# 📢 Customer Complaint Analysis Dashboard

This Tableau dashboard provides a structured view of **customer complaints data**, helping stakeholders track complaint volumes, identify product‑specific issues, and understand submission trends across regions and time. It is designed as a **portfolio project** to demonstrate skills in data visualization, KPI tracking, and actionable storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Audience
- **Audience:** Recruiters, hiring managers, and business stakeholders interested in operational performance and customer service analytics  
- **Purpose:** Showcase ability to transform raw CSV complaint data into clear, interactive dashboards  
- **Outcome:** Demonstrates proficiency in Tableau, parameter controls, and business insight communication  

---

## ❓ Business Questions Answered
1. **What is the total number of complaints received?**  
   → KPI card showing overall complaint volume  

2. **Which products generate the most complaints?**  
   → Grouped bar chart for six main product categories  

3. **How have complaints trended year‑over‑year?**  
   → Bar chart showing annual complaint counts  

4. **What are the main submission methods for complaints?**  
   → Chart breaking down request types (phone, email, web, etc.)  

5. **Where are complaints coming from geographically?**  
   → Map visualization showing state‑wise complaint distribution  

6. **What is the long‑term trend in complaints?**  
   → Line chart showing multi‑year complaint trajectory  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Product Issues:** Credit cards consistently drive higher complaint volumes, highlighting areas for quality improvement  
- **Submission Channels:** Majority of complaints are submitted via digital channels, suggesting opportunities for self‑service solutions  
- **Regional Trends:** Some states show disproportionately high complaint rates, indicating potential regional service challenges  
- **Yearly Trend:** Complaints have shown a steady decrease over multiple years since 2017, signaling positive feedback to the proactive resolution strategies  

---

## ⚙️ Techniques Used
- CSV data import and cleaning  
- Product grouping via calculated fields  
- Year filters and parameters for dynamic analysis  
- Chart types: KPI card, bar chart, map, line chart  
- Interactive filters and parameters for product groups
